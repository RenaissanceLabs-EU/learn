Featured film

What it takes to train a frontier model

15 min · Intermediate

A frontier model is a file of numbers, but producing it takes tens of trillions of tokens, tens of thousands of chips and a great deal of engineering. Using only 2026 reports (GLM-5, DeepSeek-V4, Kimi K2.5, a Meta fault-tolerance paper and Epoch AI data), the film follows data, scaling, hardware, failures, post-training and cost, and flags what is an estimate.
