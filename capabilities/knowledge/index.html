World knowledge

The model recalls facts about the world without looking them up.

Design and pre-training

Facts are absorbed from the pre-training text. What appears often is learned well, and the long tail is learned poorly.

Post-training and deployment

Fine-tuning mostly teaches the model to use what it knows. Training it on facts it does not know has been shown to increase hallucination.

2How to measure it

  • MMLUMultiple-choice questions across 57 subjects.
  • SimpleQAShort factual questions with one checkable answer.
  • TruthfulQAQuestions where a common misconception is the tempting answer.
  • FActScoreSplits a long answer into single facts and checks each one.

3Common datasets and methods

  • WikidataStructured facts, useful as a reference for grading answers.

5Findings

  • Fine-tuning on facts the model does not know increases hallucination as those facts are learned.Gekhman and colleagues
