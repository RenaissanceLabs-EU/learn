Design and pre-training

Speech models are pre-trained on very large amounts of transcribed audio.

Post-training and deployment

Fine-tuning on one language or domain lowers the error rate there. General speech needs checking again afterwards.

2How to measure it

  • FLEURSParallel speech in 102 languages.
