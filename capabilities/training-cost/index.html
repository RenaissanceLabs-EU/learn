Design and pre-training

Model size and the number of training tokens set the bill, and pre-training is nearly all of it.

Post-training and deployment

Post-training stages are small by comparison. Low-rank adapters cut the cost of fine-tuning further. Reinforcement learning pays for rollouts as well as updates.
