Resisting misuse

Design and pre-training

A base model has no notion of a request it should decline.

Post-training and deployment

Fine-tuning and preference tuning teach refusals. Filters and monitoring at deployment add a second layer.

2How to measure it

  • HarmBenchA standard setup for automated red teaming and robust refusal.

5What it costs

  • Direct fine-tuning for refusals can cause over-refusal and a drop in general ability. Low-rank adapters gave a better balance in one study.

6Findings

  • Safety-critical gate failures fell from 71 to 39 after one epoch of preference tuning.OpenEuroLLM
  • Models complied wrongly with up to 30% of requests in categories that had been studied little.Brahman and colleagues
