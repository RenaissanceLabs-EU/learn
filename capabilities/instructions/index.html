Following instructions

The model does what was asked, in the format asked for, across a conversation.

Design and pre-training

A base model continues text. It has no notion of a request or a turn.

Post-training and deployment

Supervised fine-tuning on conversations teaches the chat format and the habit of answering the question.

2How to measure it

  • IFEvalInstructions that a program can check, such as length or required words.
  • MT-BenchMulti-turn questions graded by a judge model.

3Common datasets and methods

  • UltraFeedbackModel answers with ratings, often used as preference pairs.
  • HelpSteer3Human preferences between answers.
  • FLANMany tasks rewritten as instructions.
