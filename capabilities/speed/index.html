Inference speed

The model answers quickly: a short wait for the first token, then many tokens per second.

Design and pre-training

Speed is mostly decided in the design: the model's size, its attention variant and how many parameters are active per token.

Post-training and deployment

Training does not change it. Quantisation, batching and the serving engine decide the rest.

  • Time to first token and tokens per secondMeasured on named hardware, at a stated batch size and context length.
