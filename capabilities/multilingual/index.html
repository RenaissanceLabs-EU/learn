Capabilities · Language

Many languages

The model reads and writes many languages, and answers in the language it was asked in.

Design and pre-training

Coverage is set by the pre-training text and by the tokenizer. A language that tokenizes badly starts behind.

Post-training and deployment

Post-training data is often English or machine-translated, so each stage needs its own check per language.

2How to measure it

  • BelebeleReading comprehension in 122 language variants.
  • XTREMECross-lingual tests across many languages and tasks.
  • ScandEvalA benchmarking platform for the Scandinavian languages.
  • Answers in the question's languageThe share of replies in the language of the question. Part of the hallucination benchmark.

5Findings

  • A very low score in one language (Georgian, 21.9%) was related to the tokenizer, not to the preference data.OpenEuroLLM
  • In thinking mode, one model answered 11 to 14% of non-English questions in English.OpenEuroLLM
  • Some Dutch questions were answered in German, by the base model and by the tuned one.OpenEuroLLM
