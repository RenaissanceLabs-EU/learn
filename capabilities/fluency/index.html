Capabilities · Learned from text

Fluent text

The model writes correct, natural text and predicts what comes next.

1Where it comes from

Pre-trainingMostly shaped here
Supervised fine-tuningShaped a little
Preference tuningNot shaped here
Reinforcement learningNot shaped here
Pre-training

Pre-training optimises this directly: predict the next token, over a very large body of text.

Post-training

Later stages change the style and format of the text. They do not teach the language.

2How to measure it

  • Held-out loss, or bits per byteHow surprised the model is by text it has not seen. Lower is better.
  • PalomaLanguage model fit across many separate domains.
