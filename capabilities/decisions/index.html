Calibrated decisions

The model answers a typed question with a probability that matches how often it is right.

Pre-trainingShaped a little

Pre-training

The base model supplies the reading ability. The decision itself is not there.

Post-training

A small adapter and a decision head are trained on labelled cases, then calibrated.

2How to measure it

  • Accuracy and Brier scoreBrier scores the whole probability distribution. Lower is better.
  • Expected calibration errorThe average gap between stated confidence and how often the model is right.
