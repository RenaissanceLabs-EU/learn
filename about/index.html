About Learn

A small library from Renaissance Labs on how intelligence is built, in machines and in people.

What this is

Learn collects our explainer films and a written course on training large language models. The films are rendered from code: the scenes, narration and captions all come from the scripts that sit behind each one, which is why every film has an exact transcript.

The course is for people who use or build language models and want to understand what happens before the chat window. The minds section is for anyone who is curious about how memory, emotion and relationships work. It is education, not therapy. If something here resonates because you are struggling, a clinician can help in ways a film cannot.

How we handle sources

Every film starts from a research dossier. Claims are marked as reported, estimated or unverified. Where a figure comes from a secondary summary, or an estimate is our own arithmetic, the lesson says so in the sentence. If you find an error, tell us and we will correct it and note the change.

Who makes it

Renaissance Labs is an independent lab based in Europe working on open models, open data and open evaluation, and on making that work easier to understand. The psychology films are written with a licensed clinical psychologist.
