Running on empty: understanding burnout

What it is, why it happens, five steps back

10 minBeginner11 chapters

Burnout is what happens after a long time under more pressure than you can recover from. The film explains its three parts and why it arises when work takes more than it gives back. It then walks through five steps: stop the drain, change the load, change the voice in your head, rebuild the basics and lean on other people.

What to take away

  • Researchers describe burnout as exhaustion, cynicism and feeling less effective, after long pressure without enough recovery.
  • In the demands and resources model, burnout is usually a mismatch: too much going out and too little coming in, for too long.
  • Programs aimed at the organization, such as workload and teamwork, tended to work better than programs aimed at the individual.
  • Psychological detachment, leaving work in your head as well as in your evenings, is the most studied recovery skill.
  • Withdrawing usually makes burnout heavier, and telling one person the real answer is a way to start.
