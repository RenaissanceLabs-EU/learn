Minds · Where the two meet

What is intelligence?

Compression, direction and two kinds of mind

4 minBeginner8 chapters

The film asks what a mind does that a rock does not. Its answer is that a mind finds patterns, compresses the world, keeps what matters and updates from error. It then looks at direction and honesty, compares a human brain with a language model, and ends with a mind that models itself.

What to take away

  • Predicting and compressing are two views of the same act, because the better you predict, the less you have to remember.
  • Compression is never neutral: what a mind keeps depends on what it wants.
  • The real test of intelligence is a problem it has never seen, where memory has no entry.
  • A language model trained to predict text is trained to compress it, and what it carries is that compression.
  • Both humans and models can fail at updating, one by resisting change and the other by giving way to pressure.
