Hello, old friend: living with fear

A kinder way to treat your fear

5 minBeginner9 chapters

A short, storybook-style film that treats fear as a small, loyal companion with one job: keeping you alive. It describes the two usual responses, obeying and fighting, and offers a third way: let fear come along, kneel to it as you would to a frightened child, and walk through the door together.

What to take away

  • Fear's job is protection, and it cannot tell a cliff from a job interview.
  • Obeying fear shrinks life one room at a time, and ignoring it tends to make it louder.
  • Putting a feeling into words has been found to quiet the brain's alarm response.
  • Fear does not have to leave for you to move; it can come with you, just not drive.
  • Try a hand on your chest and a sentence you would say to a child in the dark.
