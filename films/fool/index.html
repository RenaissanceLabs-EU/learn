Make a Fool of Yourself

Everything you want is on the other side

7 minBeginner11 chapters

Starting from advice Austin Butler recounted, the film looks at why embarrassment feels huge in a world of views and likes. Few people watch as closely as it feels, people are kinder than we predict, and cringe is how skill is built. It ends with a dare for the week.

What to take away

  • The fear of being judged is an old alarm, now ringing in a world of views, likes and seen markers.
  • The spotlight effect means people notice our mistakes far less than we think.
  • People who laughed at their own harmless mistakes were seen as warmer and more authentic.
  • Over a long time, people tend to regret what they never tried more than what they did.
  • Be foolish, never cruel: risk your own image, never someone else's dignity.
