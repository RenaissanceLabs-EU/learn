The alignment problem: whose values should AI follow?

Human feedback, hard choices and how to take part

8 minBeginner9 chapters

The film explains alignment as closing the gap between a capable model and one that is helpful, honest and harmless. It covers learning from human feedback and what went wrong when a model became flattering. It then walks through four open questions, the problem of whose values count, and concrete ways people can take part.

What to take away

  • A pretrained model is capable but does not know which of its possible answers people actually want, and alignment closes that gap.
  • The film describes a 2025 rollback of an overly flattering update, and draws the lesson that what we reward is what we get.
  • Medical advice, privacy, reporting users and whose side the model is on are dials that someone must set for every model.
  • The film argues that written values such as constitutions and specifications can be debated and improved, while hidden values cannot.
  • People can take part by voting, rating honestly, joining deliberations, reviewing tests in their own language and speaking up when rules are written.
