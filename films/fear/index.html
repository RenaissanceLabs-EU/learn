Fear not: the oldest advice in the world

The Litany Against Fear and what traditions say

7 minBeginner10 chapters

Starting from the Litany Against Fear in Dune, the film follows the words fear not through many traditions and finds a shared shape: the answer to fear is company, not strength. It then shows why staying lets the wave pass, and ends with a small five-step practice for the week.

What to take away

  • Under threat the brain shifts toward fast survival circuits, which is why fear can crowd out clear thinking.
  • Across many traditions the words fear not are followed by a promise of company: I am with you.
  • Having someone you love hold your hand has been found to weaken the brain's threat response.
  • Running from fear teaches the brain that running was needed; staying teaches that you can cope.
  • A small practice helps: name the fear, breathe out, tell one person, open one thing, then look at what remains.
