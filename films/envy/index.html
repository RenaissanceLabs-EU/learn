Envy is a compass

What the sting of comparison points at

6 minBeginner10 chapters

You do not envy everything, only the things you want. This film looks at why other people's good news can make you sink, the two kinds of envy researchers describe, and a way to read the feeling instead of hiding it. It ends with a small dare for one week.

What to take away

  • Envy is precise: it does not point at what is impressive, it points at what you want.
  • Researchers describe two kinds, one that pulls the other person down and one that pulls you up.
  • Ask what exactly stung, and whether you want the thing or want to be seen having it.
  • Studies of good news find that an enthusiastic response builds trust and closeness between people.
  • A small step toward the thing, even twenty minutes, tells the compass you are listening.
