A simple model of how the mind processes information

Inputs, thoughts, feelings and the models you use

7 minBeginner7 chapters

A walk through a simplified, theoretical model of cognition. Senses and feelings are inputs, thoughts form a loop, and everything is shaped by your bodily state and by your verbal models of the world. The talk ends on how changing those models changes perception.

What to take away

  • Input comes from the senses and also from unconscious feelings, which can be seen as the body communicating with you.
  • Thoughts feed more thoughts, and what the mind turns to depends on what you have been thinking about before.
  • Processing is shaped by your overall bodily state and by your models of the world, which are probably verbal.
  • A kind word is processed through those models, may change how you see yourself, and can shift both feelings and what you do.
  • Whether rain or snow feels good or bad depends on your model, so you have some room to change how you see it.
