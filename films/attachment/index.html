A secure base: the four patterns of attachment

What attachment research says about healing

14 minIntermediate13 chapters

From Bowlby and Ainsworth to adult relationships, this film walks through how attachment forms, the four infant patterns, and the two dimensions researchers use for adults. It reports the strong evidence, the modest evidence and the studies that disagree, then ends with what seems to help.

What to take away

  • Attachment in adults is better described by two dimensions, anxiety and avoidance, than by four boxes.
  • Attachment security is moderately stable over time, and the research finds no significant stability across gaps longer than fifteen years.
  • Responsiveness, noticing and answering another person's signals, is the ingredient that keeps showing up in programs that help.
  • Well-known programs have shown small or no effects in some trials, so effects are generally small to moderate.
  • Your history shaped you. It did not sentence you.
