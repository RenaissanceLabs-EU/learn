The mind is a machine that makes up stories

Why a sad day turns into a sad story

4 minBeginner6 chapters

A short talk on how the mind makes sense of the world by telling stories. Those stories are not always accurate, and a bad day can easily become a story about who you are. Once you see the pattern, you can ask what is really going on and choose a more flexible story.

What to take away

  • Everything you experience passes through the unconscious mind and then through personal models, such as models for language and for how you perceive sound.
  • The mind works to make sense of things, and a story that makes sense can win out over one that is accurate.
  • A bad day can turn into a story about yourself or the world, such as that you are a failure or that nothing works.
  • A more accurate story is often practical, for example that you feel sad because you skipped exercise or have not seen friends.
  • You will not stop making stories, but you can hold them more loosely and ask what you can do instead.
